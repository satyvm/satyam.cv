---
title: 'What I Believe'
---

Developing skill through doing, embracing quality and guiltlessly exploring my interests.

- **Clarity over complexity.** The best ideas are often the simplest ones. The work is in finding them.
- **Building beats planning.** You learn more in a week of building than a month of strategizing. Ship early, iterate often.
- **Writing is thinking.** If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough. Writing helps me refine my thoughts.
- **The margins matter.** The best insights don't come from grinding harder. They come from creating space for your subconscious to work.

Mindful that _everything around me is someone's life work._
